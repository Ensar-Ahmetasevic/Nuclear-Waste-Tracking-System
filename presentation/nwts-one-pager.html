Nuclear Waste Tracking System

Every container, accounted for.

A web application that follows radioactive waste containers from truck arrival to final storage: one record, one responsible person and one clear status at every step.

Working prototype · 2026

The problem

The trail breaks at every handover

Shipment, container and storage data are spread across paper forms, spreadsheets and separate systems. When containers move between halls and rooms, it is hard to prove who received them and when, and missing or out-of-range measurements are noticed late.

How it works

The container journey

  1. 1

    Arrival

    Truck, company, driver and plates logged on entry.

  2. 2

    Content

    Quantity, waste profile, container type and origin.

  3. 3

    Pre-storage

    Received into a hall by a named employee.

  4. 4

    Transfer

    A request moves containers from hall to room.

  5. 5

    Final storage

    Stored in a room, with stock and conditions tracked.

Three work areas cover the journey (Shipments, Pre-storage and Final storage), and each employee can be assigned to one of them.

Oversight

Conditions and stock, under watch

  • Condition alerts for temperature, radiation, humidity and pressure in every hall and room.
  • Escalation to Supervision when an alert is not handled; it closes only after a control measurement.
  • Stock reconciliation sets calculated stock beside physical counts; corrections need an administrator.
  • Traceable history of deletions, alert handling and stock checks.
